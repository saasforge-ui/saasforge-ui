# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the SaaSForge UI community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy
community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind, patient and respectful toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and any other
project channel — and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may take appropriate and fair
corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, including removing or editing comments,
commits, code, issues and other contributions, or temporarily or permanently banning a contributor.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/saasforge-ui/saasforge-ui/security/advisories/new) (describe that it
is a conduct report rather than a security issue). All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the
privacy of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers will follow these guidelines when deciding on consequences:

1. **Correction** — for inappropriate language or other unprofessional behavior: a private, written warning
   explaining the violation, and possibly a request for a public apology.
2. **Warning** — for a single serious incident or a series of actions: a warning with consequences for continued
   behavior, including no interaction with the people involved for a period of time.
3. **Temporary ban** — for a serious violation or sustained inappropriate behavior: a temporary ban from any
   interaction with the community.
4. **Permanent ban** — for a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or
   disparagement of classes of individuals.
